How it goes

Our new homes & custom building process

  1. 1

    Plan & permit

    Drawings and engineering settled, permits filed, budget agreed before a shovel moves.

  2. 2

    Site & foundation

    Lot cleared, excavation to the drawings, footings below frost line and walls up square.

  3. 3

    Framing

    Floors, walls and roof structure go up a storey at a time, inspected as we go.

  4. 4

    Close in & rough-in

    Roof, windows and doors seal the shell; plumbing, electrical and HVAC run before anything is covered.

  5. 5

    Insulate & board

    Insulated, vapour-sealed and drywalled — the house becomes rooms.

  6. 6

    Finish & hand over

    Floors, kitchen, baths, trim, paint. Punch list closed, keys handed over.
