How it goes

How design–build works

  1. 1

    Brief & budget

    What you need, what it should cost, and what the building will allow — settled together.

    The first meeting is about what you need the building to do, what you can spend, and what the site and structure will allow. We put those three things in one conversation from the start, so the design that follows is one you can afford to build. You get a written brief and a target budget before any drawings are made.

  2. 2

    Design & engineering

    Drawings developed with the build in mind, engineered and stamped.

    Our designers and engineers develop the drawings with the people who will build them looking over their shoulder. That means fewer surprises, details that can actually be built, and a price that tracks the design as it develops. Plans are engineered and stamped, and you see cost impacts as choices are made — not after.

  3. 3

    Permits

    Filed, chased, and in hand before work starts.

    We prepare and file the permit application, respond to the municipality's comments, and chase it through. Zoning relief, stormwater, historic review and utility applications are handled by us where they apply. Work does not start until the permit is in hand.

  4. 4

    Build

    One site team, one schedule, every trade sequenced by us.

    One site team and one schedule. We sequence every trade — excavation, concrete, framing, roofing, mechanicals, finishes — and are on site to coordinate them. One person answers your calls and knows the whole job. Changes are priced in writing before they are built.

  5. 5

    Inspections

    Rough-in and final, booked and passed as part of the job.

    Rough-in and final inspections for building, electrical, plumbing and mechanical are booked by us, prepared for and passed. Where a third-party inspection or engineer's letter is needed, we arrange it. You never have to chase an inspector.

  6. 6

    Handover

    Walkthrough, punch list closed, documentation handed over.

    A full walkthrough with you, a punch list written down and closed out, then the handover: warranties, manuals, as-built drawings, permit close-out documents, and the contacts for anything you need later. One company accountable from the first sketch to the last screw.
