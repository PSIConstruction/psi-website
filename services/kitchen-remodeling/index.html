How it goes

How we build a kitchen

  1. 1

    Design & layout

    Work triangle, storage, appliances and lighting planned on paper before anything is ordered.

    We measure the room and talk through how you cook, store and gather. The layout is designed around the work triangle, traffic through the room, storage you will actually use, appliances and their clearances, and lighting — task, ambient and accent. You see it in drawings or a 3D rendering and we price it against those before anything is ordered.

  2. 2

    Order & schedule

    Cabinets, stone and appliances ordered to lead times so nothing waits on anything.

    Cabinets, countertop stone, appliances, tile, plumbing fixtures and lighting all have different lead times; we order them in the right sequence and build the schedule around the longest one. Demolition does not start until the cabinets have a delivery date, so the kitchen is never out of service waiting on a box.

  3. 3

    Demolition

    Old kitchen out, services capped, floor protected.

    The old kitchen comes out in a day or two. Plumbing and gas are capped, electrical made safe, floors that are staying are protected, and dust is controlled with barriers and negative air where it matters. We set up a temporary kitchen space with you if you are living in the house.

  4. 4

    Rough-in

    Plumbing and electrical moved to the new plan, inspected, then closed.

    Plumbing and electrical are moved to the new plan — supplies and drain for a relocated sink, dedicated circuits for appliances, under-cabinet and pendant wiring, venting for the hood. Framing changes for a new window or a removed wall happen here. Everything is inspected before it is closed.

  5. 5

    Cabinets & counters

    Cabinets set level and true, template made, stone cut and installed.

    Cabinets are set level, plumb and screwed to the framing, with fillers and panels scribed to the walls. A template is made for the stone, which is cut and installed with the sink and cooktop cut-outs in place. Backsplash height, outlet positions and appliance fit are all checked against the counters.

  6. 6

    Tile, lighting, hardware

    Backsplash, pendants, pulls — and the first meal.

    Backsplash tile, pendants and recessed lighting trimmed out, handles and pulls, toe kicks and crown, appliances installed and tested, plumbing connected. A punch-list walk with you, and the first meal cooked in it.
