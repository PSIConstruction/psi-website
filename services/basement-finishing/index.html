How it goes

Our basement finishing process

  1. 1

    Moisture & egress check

    Walls, slab and drainage assessed; egress windows planned where bedrooms are going.

  2. 2

    Layout & permits

    Rooms laid out around the structure and services that can't move. Permit filed.

  3. 3

    Framing & rough-in

    Stud walls up, drain lines cut into the slab where a bathroom needs them, electrical and HVAC run.

  4. 4

    Insulate & board

    Insulated against the block, vapour-controlled, drywalled and taped.

  5. 5

    Bathroom & floors

    Tile, tub or shower, vanity; flooring run wall to wall.

  6. 6

    Light, paint, hand over

    Recessed lighting, paint, doors and trim. A floor you'd never call a basement.
