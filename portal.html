What we stand for, and how we show it

Communication. Quality. Straightforwardness.

Most contractors go quiet between site visits. We don’t. The way we build a quality job while being upfront with you is through how we communicate — and that runs through a client portal built for your project alone, open to you at any time.

  • Communication

    An update every working day, with photographs. Decisions we need from you, with the date we need them by. Nothing waits for the next time we happen to meet.

  • Quality

    You see the substrate, the waterproofing, the framing — the parts that get covered up — in the daily photographs before the finish goes on.

  • Straightforwardness

    The contract, every change order, every allowance and what has been spent against it, every invoice and receipt: in one place, in the open, not in an email thread.
